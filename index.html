About Me

I am a Software Engineer at Tencent in Shenzhen, working on LLM serving optimization. Previously, I was a Software Engineer at Google CoreML in Sunnyvale, CA, where I worked with the Edge AI team after completing an internship with TPU XLA team.

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from Purdue University in 2024, advised by Prof.Dongyan Xu and Prof.Antonio Bianchi. My Ph.D. research focuses on AI compilers, specifically the application of program analysis techniques. I received my B.Sc. degree in Computer Science from Sun Yat-sen University in 2018, awarded with two National Scholarships (top 2% in the department) in 2015 and 2017.
